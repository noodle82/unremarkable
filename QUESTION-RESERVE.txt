UNREMARKABLE! — additional question ideas
Saved alongside the 50-question master; not yet in the playable bank.

1. How many McDonald’s restaurants are in Wales?
   Needs a dated restaurant count and source, because openings change it.
2. Roughly how many beans are in a 415 g tin of Heinz Beanz?
   Needs a sourced estimate; actual bean counts vary between tins.
3. How many times is the phrase “Get back” sung in the Beatles song?
   Needs a named recording/version and a rule for overlapping vocals.
4. How many sports at the Paris 2024 Olympics involved a ball?
   Pin to Paris 2024; define sports versus disciplines and whether a shuttlecock counts.
5. How many cats have lived at 10 Downing Street?
   Needs a date range and a rule for official mousers versus all resident cats.
