How to be unremarkable

Read the room.
Leave brilliance at the door.

01

Guess in secret.

Pass the device around. Everyone enters a number. Knowing the real answer is strictly optional.

02

Find your middle ground.

The winning middle guess gets +2. Highest and lowest lose 1. Nail the rounded group average for a bonus point.

03

To the Unremarkulator!

Watch the bars rise, collect your modest glory and carry on. The double-points finale can change everything.

What are the rounds?

Heights, Sizes & Distances; Everyday Numbers; History & Records; Ordinary Life; Extraordinary Circumstances; Know Your Players; then the fixed double-points finale.

One question per category. The finale always asks you to pick a number between 1 and 1,000,000. The highest total score wins.
