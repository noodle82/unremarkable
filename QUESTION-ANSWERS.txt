UNREMARKABLE! — master question and answer key
Actual answers are for interest. Scores depend on the players’ guesses.

HEIGHTS & SIZES

1. How tall is a New Routemaster double-decker London bus, approximately? [metres]
Answer: About 4.4 metres
This question refers to the New Routemaster rather than every model of London bus.
Source: Transport for London — Big Red Book
https://www.transportforall.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/bigredbook2014.pdf

2. How many metres long was the Titanic, approximately? [metres]
Answer: About 269 metres
Titanic’s overall length was approximately 269.1 metres.
Source: Causeway Coast and Glens Museum Services — Titanic
https://www.niarchive.org/projectitems/rms-titanics-causeway-connections/

3. Roughly how tall can a fully grown male giraffe be? [metres]
Answer: About 5.5 metres
Adult males can reach around 5.5 metres. This is an upper-end height, rather than an average for all giraffes.
Source: San Diego Zoo — giraffe
https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/giraffe

4. To the nearest metre, how long was the longest blue whale documented by Guinness World Records? [metres]
Answer: About 34 metres
Guinness lists a female measured in South Georgia in 1909 at 33.57 metres. Rounded to the nearest whole metre, that is 34.
Source: Guinness World Records — largest whale
https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/69467-largest-whale

5. How many centimetres tall is the Mona Lisa painting, excluding its frame? [cm]
Answer: 77 cm
The Louvre lists the painting as 77 cm high and 53 cm wide. The frame is not included.
Source: Musée du Louvre — educational guide
https://api-www.louvre.fr/sites/default/files/2025-11/Dossier_pedagogique_Louvre%20en%20classe.pdf

6. How many centimetres long is the Statue of Liberty’s nose, approximately? [cm]
Answer: About 137 cm
The US National Park Service lists 4 feet 6 inches, equivalent to 137.16 cm. Rounded to the nearest centimetre: 137 cm.
Source: US National Park Service — Statue statistics
https://home.nps.gov/stli/learn/historyculture/statue-statistics.htm

7. How tall is the Leaning Tower of Pisa above the ground, approximately? [metres]
Answer: About 56 metres
Pisa’s municipal tourism site gives about 56 metres above ground. Height varies with the side measured; the monument operator’s larger 58.36-metre figure uses a different measurement reference.
Source: Comune di Pisa — tourism
https://turismo.pisa.it/cultura/dettaglio/piazza-del-duomo-la-torre-pendente/

8. How many metres across is one clock dial on the tower housing Big Ben? [metres]
Answer: 7 metres
Each of the four clock dials is seven metres in diameter. Big Ben itself is the bell.
Source: UK Parliament — Big Ben facts
https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/big-ben/facts-figures/

9. How tall is the Burj Khalifa in Dubai? [metres]
Answer: 828 metres
The architectural height, including the spire.
Source: Burj Khalifa — official fact sheet
https://www.burjkhalifa.ae/en/assets/FACT-SHEET.pdf

10. How tall is Al Pacino, approximately, in centimetres? [cm]
Answer: About 168 cm
IMDb lists his height as 5 feet 6 inches (1.68 metres). Celebrity heights are reported figures rather than independently certified measurements, and listings can differ.
Source: IMDb — Al Pacino biography
https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000199/bio/

DISTANCES

11. As the crow flies, how far is it from London to Timbuktu, approximately? [km]
Answer: About 3,860 km
A great-circle distance rather than a road route; exact figures vary slightly with the chosen points in each city.
Source: Travelmath — London to Timbuktu
https://www.travelmath.com/distance/from/London,+United+Kingdom/to/Timbuktu,+Mali

12. As the crow flies, roughly how far apart are the university cities of Oxford and Cambridge? [km]
Answer: About 107 km
Approximate straight-line distance between the cities, not a road journey. The universities have many buildings, so this uses city reference points rather than an unspecified pair of campuses.
Source: Travelmath — Oxford to Cambridge
https://www.travelmath.com/distance/from/Oxford,+United+Kingdom/to/Cambridge,+United+Kingdom

13. At its widest point, how wide is the Grand Canyon from rim to rim, to the nearest kilometre? [km]
Answer: About 29 km
The US National Park Service gives a maximum rim-to-rim width of 18 miles (about 29 km). This is not the width of the Colorado River below.
Source: US National Park Service — Grand Canyon statistics
https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/management/statistics.htm

14. For the widest standard UK double yellow road lines, how wide is the gap between the two painted lines? [cm]
Answer: 10 cm
The prescribed line widths are 50, 75 or 100 mm, with the gap matching the width of each line. The widest standard version therefore has a 100 mm (10 cm) gap. Narrower versions have smaller gaps.
Source: Department for Transport — Traffic Signs Manual, Chapter 3, section 13.4.5
https://www.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/223943/traffic-signs-manual-chapter-03.pdf

15. At its narrowest point, roughly how many kilometres of sea separate the UK and France? [km]
Answer: About 34 km
Dover District Council describes the narrowest part of the Dover Strait as 21 miles, or roughly 34 km. More precise endpoint measurements are often given as around 33 km. This refers to the British and French mainland coasts.
Source: Dover District Council — Channel crossings exhibition
https://www.dover.gov.uk/News/Press-Releases/2023/New-Dover-Museum-Exhibition-Charts-History-of-Channel-Crossings.aspx

16. How many metres long is Sydney Harbour Bridge, including its approaches? [metres]
Answer: 1,149 metres
This is the full length including approaches, rather than just the central arch span.
Source: Transport for NSW — bridge facts
https://www.transport.nsw.gov.au/system/files/media/documents/2024/Online_241205%20MR%20Graham%20Bridge%20maintenance.pdf

17. How long is Brighton Palace Pier, approximately? [metres]
Answer: About 525 metres
The pier is 1,722 feet long, equivalent to approximately 525 metres.
Source: Visit Brighton — Palace Pier
https://www.visitbrighton.com/things-to-do/brighton-palace-pier-p60603

18. How long is the Channel Tunnel, approximately? [km]
Answer: About 50.5 km
LeShuttle gives this approximate length between Folkestone and Calais.
Source: LeShuttle — Channel Tunnel facts
https://www.leshuttle.com/uk-en/discover/why-choose-leshuttle/build/the-channel-tunnel?viewmode=0

19. To the nearest metre, how far must a ten-pin bowling ball travel from the foul line to the front pin? [metres]
Answer: About 18 metres
The regulation distance is 60 feet, or 18.288 metres, from the foul line to the centre of the headpin. The run-up is not included.
Source: Professional Bowlers Association — bowling lingo
https://www.pba.com/about/bowling-lingo

20. What is the average distance from Earth to the Moon, approximately? [km]
Answer: About 384,400 km
An average distance between Earth and the Moon. The actual separation varies during the orbit.
Source: NASA — Moon facts
https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/

EVERYDAY NUMBERS

21. How many pages are in the original 1997 UK edition of Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone? [pages]
Answer: 223 pages
The original Bloomsbury UK edition is specified: later editions, illustrated editions and translations have different page counts.

https://www.bada.org/object/j-k-rowling-harry-potter-and-philosophers-stone-first-edition

There is no capsule numbered 13, so the numbering runs up to 33 even though there are only 32.
Source: London Eye — anniversary facts
https://www.londoneye.com/25th-anniversary/

23. How many grams does one standard UK Cadbury Creme Egg weigh? [grams]
Answer: 40 grams
The standard UK single Creme Egg, rather than a mini egg or another market’s version. Pack sizes can change.
Source: Cadbury — standard Creme Egg
https://www.cadbury.co.uk/products/cadbury-creme-egg-40g/

Answer: 775 rooms
The total includes state rooms, bedrooms, offices and bathrooms.
Source: The Royal Family — Buckingham Palace
https://www.royal.uk/royal-residences-buckingham-palace?_amp=true

25. How many dimples are on a 2025 Titleist Pro V1 golf ball? [dimples]

26. How many grams does a standard modern UK £1 coin weigh? [grams]
Answer: 8.75 grams
The circulating twelve-sided pound coin, introduced in 2017, weighs 8.75 g. The older round pound coin had a different weight.
Source: The Royal Mint — twelve-sided £1 coin
https://www.royalmint.com/new-pound-coin/

27. How many pieces are in LEGO’s 135 cm-long Titanic model, set 10294? [pieces]
Answer: 9,090 pieces
The model is approximately 1:200 scale and over a metre long.
Source: LEGO — large building sets
https://www.lego.com/en-se/categories/adults-welcome/article/biggest-lego-sets-ever-made

28. What is the maximum certified passenger seating capacity of an Airbus A380? [passengers]
Answer: 853 passengers
This is the certified maximum for an all-economy arrangement; airline layouts usually have fewer seats.
Source: Airbus — A380 facts and figures
https://www.airbus.com/sites/g/files/jlcbta136/files/2021-10/EN-Airbus-A380-Facts-and-Figures_0.pdf?pubDate=20251126

29. How many upright stones was Stonehenge’s outer stone circle designed to contain? [stones]
Answer: 30 upright stones
This asks about the original outer sarsen circle, excluding its horizontal lintels and the inner stones; counting every surviving fragment gives a different question.
Source: English Heritage — stones of Stonehenge
https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/stonehenge/things-to-do/stone-circle/stones-of-stonehenge/

30. How many times does the word “God” appear in the New Testament of the King James Bible? [mentions]
Answer: 1,354 mentions (reference count)
Christian Bible Reference lists 1,354. We use its base-word count: capitalisation is ignored; “gods” and “God’s”, headings and footnotes are excluded. Counts can vary with the digital text and search method, so this is the named reference’s count rather than a universal total.
Source: Christian Bible Reference — word-count table
https://www.christianbiblereference.org/faq_WordCount.htm

HISTORY & RECORDS

31. In what year did the first McDonald’s restaurant open in the UK? [year]
Answer: 1974
The first UK restaurant opened in Woolwich, south London.
Source: McDonald’s UK — first restaurant
https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/help/faq/when-did-mcdonald-s-first-restaurant-open-in-the-u-k.html

32. In what year did Percy Spencer invent microwave cooking and file the patent that led to the microwave oven? [year]
Answer: 1945
Spencer discovered the heating effect and the patent application was filed in October 1945. The first commercial Radarange followed in 1947; the patent was granted in 1950.
Source: MIT Lemelson — Percy Spencer
https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/percy-spencer

33. In what year was the first video uploaded to YouTube? [year]
Answer: 2005
“Me at the zoo” was uploaded on 23 April 2005.
Source: YouTube — tenth anniversary
https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/celebrating-10-years-of-youtube/

34. How many seconds did the Wright brothers’ first successful powered flight last? [seconds]
Answer: 12 seconds
Orville Wright flew for 12 seconds on 17 December 1903, covering about 120 feet. Their later flights that day lasted longer.
Source: US National Park Service — Wright Flyer
https://home.nps.gov/articles/wrightflyer.htm

35. What was the highest official race speed-trap reading at the 2016 Formula 1 Mexican Grand Prix, to the nearest mph? [mph]
Answer: 231 mph
Valtteri Bottas recorded 372.5 km/h in a Mercedes-powered Williams FW38. That converts to about 231.46 mph, or 231 mph to the nearest whole number. This specifies the official race speed trap, rather than private testing or unofficial telemetry.
Source: FIA — 2016 Mexican Grand Prix race speed trap
https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/speed_trap_37.pdf

36. How many lifeboats did the Titanic carry on its maiden voyage? [lifeboats]

The total includes the collapsible boats.
Source: Causeway Coast and Glens Museum Services — Titanic
https://www.niarchive.org/projectitems/rms-titanics-causeway-connections/

https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/icons/robert-wadlow-the-tallest-man-ever

https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/oldest-person

39. How many hot dogs with buns did Joey Chestnut eat in ten minutes in his 2024 Netflix showdown? [hot dogs]
