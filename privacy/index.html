Privacy in simple words

Your game stays on the device you share. We do not receive your guesses or scores through an online game service.

Use a nickname. Do not enter your address, school, phone number or other private details in a player name or send them in a screenshot.

Names are used only while you play. The player list starts empty when you open Who’s playing? again. Refreshing or leaving the game loses the current game. Play again can keep the same group for another game on that page.

If you email us, we see your email address and what you send. A trusted adult can help you contact us; you can also ask us a privacy question yourself.

What the game handles on your device

The game processes player names or nicknames, numerical guesses, scores, the current question, round progress and music/sound-effect choices locally. It does not ask for an email address, date of birth, account, location or contact list to play.

The game code does not upload names, guesses or scores. They are not used for advertising. Other people using or watching the shared device can see what is displayed. Your browser, device management and any backup arrangements you use can also affect the privacy of locally stored information.

For the final-score tie-break, the entered name is compared with a public baby-name list bundled inside the game. There is no online name lookup and no attempt to identify you. This playful result has no legal or similarly significant effect.

Cookies, browser storage and your choices

The current game does not save player names, guesses, scores, progress or sound choices in cookies, local storage or session storage. They are held only in the current page’s memory. There are no player accounts or cloud saves.

Who’s playing? opens with an empty list. Play again keeps the same group for another game while that page remains open. Choosing Change players opens a fresh list. Refreshing or confirming Exit game discards the current game. Music returns to off and sound effects to on when the page reloads.

Older versions saved names under the browser-storage entry unremarkable-names. When this updated game loads, it attempts to remove that one old entry for the current site. It does not read the saved names or clear unrelated storage. If browser storage is blocked, the game still works.

If you played an older version at a different address, this site cannot clear that address’s storage. You can remove legacy data using your browser’s site-data controls for that address. We cannot remotely recover or erase information held only on your device.

The game’s own code does not set cookies or include advertising trackers, analytics, social-media embeds or third-party fonts. These Terms, Privacy and Support pages contain no scripts, forms or browser-storage code. Music, sound effects and the public name-count list are bundled with the game.

Website hosting and ordinary web requests

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it records visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub. Loading the website also sends normal request information to the host; it is different from sending us your guesses or scores.

GitHub explains its handling, retention and international transfers in its Privacy Statement. We do not run a separate visitor analytics service. GoDaddy supplies the domain registration; website hosting remains with GitHub.

Following a question’s source link takes you to an external service with its own privacy practices.

If you email support

When you email support@bluffle.co.uk, we receive your email address, message, attachments and normal email delivery information. We use this to answer your enquiry, investigate bugs, handle privacy requests and keep necessary resolution records. You do not need to email us to play.

Mark Atkin handles support personally. The mailbox is provided through GoDaddy using Microsoft’s Outlook email service. The same mailbox serves Bluffle and UNREMARKABLE!; messages are used to deal with the enquiry you send.

For routine support, our lawful basis is legitimate interests: helping people who contact us, maintaining a functioning game and protecting users. We use only information needed for those purposes and take particular care with children’s information. For the local game functions, our legitimate interest is providing the on-device game that people choose to use. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Where an identified legal obligation requires particular processing, that obligation is the lawful basis. Correspondence needed for legal claims is kept only where justified by the relevant legal need.

We do not sell support messages or use them for marketing. Please remove player names and other personal details from screenshots and never send passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary information about children.

How long support information is kept

Routine support messages are kept while the enquiry is open and for up to 12 months after closure, then deleted or anonymised. This is the support policy also used for Bluffle, not a period prescribed by law.

Relevant records may be kept longer where needed for a specific legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. We limit what is kept, record the reason and review the need.

Messages removed from the mailbox may remain temporarily in provider recovery systems or backups under the provider’s arrangements. GitHub’s hosting records and current-session game information have the separate retention arrangements described above.

Providers and overseas processing

GitHub, GoDaddy and Microsoft operate internationally. Website request information and support correspondence may be processed outside the UK, including in the United States. We do not promise UK-only storage.

The providers describe transfer safeguards in their privacy information and applicable data-processing terms. Depending on the service and destination, these may include recognised adequacy arrangements and contractual safeguards with UK-specific terms where required. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your correspondence or how to obtain a copy.

Your privacy rights

You may ask for access to personal information we hold, correction, deletion, restriction and, where applicable, portability. Rights depend on the circumstances and have legal limits. Contact support@bluffle.co.uk; there is normally no charge. We normally respond within one month, subject to lawful exceptions and extensions.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Tell us your circumstances and we will consider your request and explain the outcome.

We may ask for proportionate information to confirm we are dealing with the correct person. Children have privacy rights too, and we can explain them in accessible language or assist an appropriate representative.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. You do not need to contact us first. Information held only on your device must be removed using that device’s controls.

Payments and future changes

This version has no payments, subscriptions, accounts, advertising or online multiplayer. We receive no payment-card information through the game.

Before introducing materially different data handling, we will update this notice and provide any required information and choices. The date above shows when this notice was prepared.
