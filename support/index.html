Get in touch

Email support

Use support@bluffle.co.uk and put UNREMARKABLE! in the subject so we can distinguish it from Bluffle enquiries. If the button does not open an email app, copy the address into your usual email service.

Mark Atkin handles support personally and aims to reply as soon as reasonably possible; there is no fixed support-response promise.

Include the device and browser you use, what you expected and the steps that caused the problem. For a question correction, include its wording and category, and a source if available. Remove player names and private information from screenshots. Do not send passwords or payment-card information. Younger players can ask a trusted adult for help; privacy questions are welcome directly too.

What is available now?

The current free game has 3–12 players, five random category rounds drawn from 50 questions, a fixed double-points finale, music, sound effects, Exit game and Change question. There are no player accounts, advertisements, purchases, online multiplayer or cloud game recovery.

The game runs locally after the website loads. The embedded music, effects and baby-name tie-break do not require extra online lookups during play. The website still needs a connection to load again.

Common questions

How do I win?

Don’t guess the answer, guess your opponents. Aim for the middle. Normally the middle wins +2 and losing extremes receive −1. An exact hit on the average rounded to a whole number earns +1 independently. The finale doubles all of these. Open How to be unremarkable on the home screen for the full rules.

Why didn’t both middle guesses win?

With an even group, the two middle values are compared with the unrounded average of everyone’s guesses. The closer middle value wins. Equal distances share the award; matching winning guesses also share. The rounded-average bonus is a separate calculation.

Why did the most popular name win a tied score?

The tie-break is intentionally arbitrary: among the players tied for the highest total, the game compares entered first names with the bundled ONS England and Wales 2025 counts. Equal leading counts or no listed names go to a random draw. The result explains the decision. There is no online name lookup or assessment of your identity.

I can’t hear the sound effects or music.

Tap a game button, then open Settings. Sound effects are on by default and music is off. Their switches are independent. Check device volume and mute settings. Music is intentionally much quieter than the effects. It pauses when the page is hidden and resumes on return if enabled. Refreshing restores the default switches.

Why are there no “Meh” or “Uh-oh” voices?

Blue and red bars now end with their boinks only, following the shared low musical rise. The green winner keeps its ta-da, Yay and cheering. The winner’s name enlarges once, in time with Yay.

Can we get another question?

Choose Change question before the reveal in rounds 1–5. It picks another question in the same category and starts that round again for everyone. Earlier scores stay. You will be asked before entered guesses are discarded. The fixed final question cannot be changed.

How do I leave or start again?

Choose Exit game and confirm. Current names, guesses and scores are discarded, and you return home. Refreshing also starts over. Play again at the final results keeps the group for another game on that page; Change players opens a fresh list.

Are player names saved?

No. Names stay only in the current page session. Who’s playing? opens with an empty list. Replay can keep your group for another game while the same page is open. Refreshing or exiting discards the current game.

This version also removes its own old saved-name entry when it loads. If you used an older version at another address, clear that address’s site data in your browser to remove its legacy names.

The page seems to be an older version.

Refresh after a website update has finished deploying. On a Windows browser, Ctrl+F5 can help. On a phone, close and reopen the tab. Clearing website data is not normally necessary, but can remove entries left by older versions. A refresh always loses an in-progress game.

Can I recover a game on another device?

No. There is no account, cloud sync or game-recovery service. Names, guesses and scores stay only in the current page session and are not saved between visits.

How do I report an unsuitable or inaccurate question?

Email the question wording, category and a short explanation. Factual answers can depend on the edition, date, measurement or counting method stated in the question. Wild Cards are hypothetical. Do not perform a hypothetical challenge to answer it.

Is the game accessible?

The game provides keyboard controls, separate sound switches, a Skip animation control and support for reduced-motion preferences. If something prevents you playing, send the device, browser and the step that causes difficulty. Please share only the accessibility information needed to explain the issue.

Privacy requests and complaints

Contact support@bluffle.co.uk for personal information held in support correspondence or a complaint about the service. We cannot remotely access or erase information stored only on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for your rights, support-message retention and provider information.
