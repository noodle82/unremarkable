The current free game

UNREMARKABLE! is a local pass-and-play party game for 3–12 players sharing one device. The aim is to read the other players’ guesses and finish in the middle. The factual answer is revealed for interest; closeness to that answer does not determine points.

A game has six rounds: Heights, Sizes & Distances; Everyday Numbers; History & Records; Ordinary Life; Extraordinary Circumstances; and a fixed double-points finale. The first five rounds draw from 75 free questions, with 15 in each category. The finale asks players to pick a whole number from 1 to 1,000,000.

Normal rounds award +2 for the winning middle value, −1 for each losing extreme and an independent +1 for an exact hit on the rounded average. An even group’s middle values are compared with the unrounded average; equal distances can share the award. All awards and penalties double in the finale. The in-game instructions explain ties and scoring in detail.

The game is intended for personal, non-commercial entertainment. There are no accounts, online multiplayer, cloud game backups or money prizes in this version.

Playing fairly and safely

Use nicknames where possible and respect the other players. Do not put private, offensive or harmful information in a player name, use the game unlawfully, disrupt the service or try to access someone else’s device or information without permission.

Other people sharing or watching the device can see information displayed on it. Hidden guesses are a pass-and-play feature, not a security system. Adults should help younger players choose suitable questions; Change question is available in the first five rounds.

Hypothetical Wild Cards are for discussion and guessing. They are not instructions to perform a challenge, eat a particular amount or put yourself at risk.

Questions, answers and the name decider

Questions and answers are entertainment, not professional advice. Some answers are estimates or depend on a specified edition, model, date or counting method. Source links and explanation notes appear after factual reveals. Please report a suspected error with the question wording and category.

A tied final score uses an intentionally playful baby-name popularity decider based on the bundled ONS England and Wales 2025 lists. It compares entered names with published counts on the device. It is not an assessment of a person, their identity, gender or worth. Equal leading counts or no published matches go to a random draw. Scores remain tied numerically; the decider awards the crown.

Third-party product names and references identify question subjects. They do not imply those organisations sponsor or endorse the game.

Storage, updates and availability

Player names, guesses, scores, progress and sound choices stay in the current page session; they are not saved between visits. Who’s playing? starts with an empty list. Play again keeps the group for another game on that page. Change players opens a fresh list. Refreshing or confirming Exit game discards the current game. There is no cloud recovery.

Music defaults off and sound effects default on when the page reloads. The website needs a connection to load; after loading, gameplay and its embedded audio run locally. An offline reload of the website is not guaranteed.
